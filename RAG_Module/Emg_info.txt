EMERGENCY INFORMATION ASSISTANT

IMPORTANT DISCLAIMER:
This chatbot provides general emergency information and basic safety guidance. It is not a replacement for trained emergency professionals. In a life-threatening situation, contact the appropriate emergency service immediately.

EMERGENCY CONTACT NUMBERS – INDIA:
112 - National Emergency Number
108 - Ambulance / Emergency Medical Service
101 - Fire and Rescue Service
100 - Police
1098 - Child Helpline
181 - Women Helpline

MEDICAL EMERGENCY:
If a person is unconscious, having severe difficulty breathing, or has heavy bleeding, call 112 or 108 immediately. Keep the person safe and avoid giving food or water to an unconscious person. Follow instructions from emergency medical professionals.

HEAVY BLEEDING:
Call emergency medical services. Apply firm pressure to the wound using clean cloth or gauze. Continue applying pressure until medical help arrives. Do not remove an object deeply embedded in the wound.

BURNS:
Move away from the source of the burn. Cool the affected area with clean running water for about 20 minutes. Do not apply ice, toothpaste, oil, or butter. Seek medical help for serious or extensive burns.

FIRE EMERGENCY:
Immediately move away from the fire and alert people nearby. Call 101 or 112. Use stairs instead of elevators. If there is smoke, stay low and move toward a safe exit. Do not return to a burning building.

GAS LEAK:
Do not switch electrical appliances or lights on or off. Do not use matches or flames. If it is safe, close the gas supply and move outside. Call emergency services or the gas emergency service from a safe location.

ROAD ACCIDENT:
Move to a safe location if possible and call 112 or 108. Do not unnecessarily move an injured person, especially if a spinal injury is suspected. Provide basic assistance only if it is safe to do so.

ELECTRICAL SHOCK:
Do not touch a person who is still in contact with an electrical source. Switch off the electricity if it can be done safely. Call 112 or 108. Seek medical attention even if the person appears to recover.

DROWNING:
Call 112 or 108 immediately. If the person is safely reachable, remove them from the water without putting yourself at risk. Check breathing and follow emergency medical instructions.

POISONING:
Do not make the person vomit unless instructed by a medical professional. Keep the substance or container available for medical professionals if possible. Contact emergency medical services immediately.

NATURAL DISASTER:
During earthquakes, move away from windows and heavy objects and protect your head. During floods, move to higher ground and avoid walking or driving through floodwater. Follow instructions from local authorities.

MISSING PERSON:
Contact the police and provide recent photographs, identifying information, clothing details, and the last known location. For a missing child, contact the police and Child Helpline 1098.

POLICE EMERGENCY:
For immediate danger, crime, violence, or threats to safety, call 112 or 100. Move to a safe location and provide your location and relevant information to the police.

WOMEN'S SAFETY:
If you are in immediate danger, call 112. Move to a safe public place and contact a trusted person. Women can also contact the Women Helpline at 181.

CHILD EMERGENCY:
For a child facing danger, abuse, or requiring urgent assistance, contact 112 or Child Helpline 1098.

GENERAL EMERGENCY RULE:
Stay calm, move to a safe location, identify the type of emergency, contact the appropriate emergency service, and follow instructions from trained professionals.

UNKNOWN EMERGENCY:
If the emergency is unclear or the situation is life-threatening, call 112 immediately and describe your location and situation clearly.
